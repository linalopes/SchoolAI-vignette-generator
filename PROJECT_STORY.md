## Full Version (README intro / press)

**School of Tomorrow's AI Vignette Generator** is a gesture-driven animation tool built with p5.js. It creates elegant intro and outro vignettes for video content—the kind of visual signature that appears before a YouTube video or at the end of an Instagram Reel.

The process begins with a simple act: drawing a line. Using click-and-drag, you sketch a rough path across the canvas. This raw gesture—full of the natural imperfections of human input—is then transformed through **George Chaikin's corner-cutting algorithm**, a technique from 1974 that recursively smooths polylines by "cutting" their corners. The result is a flowing curve that preserves the spirit of your gesture while refining its form.

As the curve animates, it draws itself across the screen, accompanied by **Perlin noise-driven splashes**—organic dots that bloom and drift along the path like ink dispersing in water. The noise isn't random; it's continuous, creating motion that feels alive rather than chaotic.

When the path completes its journey, a **translucent pink circle** moves to the center and expands, creating a soft backdrop. The title—*School of Tomorrow's AI*—appears letter by letter in a typewriter effect, resolving the animation into a clean, branded moment.

The project supports both **landscape (16:9)** and **portrait (9:16)** formats, with exports to **SVG** (true vector) and **WebM video** at up to 4K resolution. All drawing data is stored in normalized coordinates, ensuring the same composition looks consistent whether previewed at 800px or recorded at 3840px.

At its heart, this is a project about **transformation**: the transformation of gesture into curve, of noise into pattern, of raw creative input into polished output. It's a small poem about how algorithms can serve—rather than replace—human expression.
